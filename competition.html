Competition Rules, Regulations & Definitions

Scoring Criteria

  • 20% Timing: Latin Hustle ("and 1-2-3") or Salsa (1-2-3, 5-6-7). For salsa, ON 1 or ON 2 timing must be consistent throughout.
  • 15% Musicality: Creative interpretation through patterns, footwork, hits, and tricks.
  • 15% Technique: Balance, placement, line, and proper weight changes. Movement should be clear and effortless.
  • 15% Difficulty: Complexity of patterns, turns, shines, tricks, dips, drops, and flares.
  • 15% Partnering/Connection: Lead/follow connection and synchronicity during side-by-sides.
  • 10% Choreography: Musical interpretation, space usage, and originality.
  • 10% Overall Presentation: Costuming, showmanship, and sportsmanship.

General Rules

  • Partnerships: One male and one female unless designated as same gender division
  • Time Limits:
    • Maximum 2.5 minutes total
    • Solos: 1.5 minutes preferred
    • Couples: 2 minutes preferred
    • Teams: 2.5-3 minutes preferred
  • Props are not allowed
  • Music must be uploaded via website

Division-Specific Rules

  • Cabaret Divisions:
    • Tricks allowed
    • Must be 50% recognizable salsa
    • Consistent ON1 or ON2 timing required
  • Classic Showcase:
    • One foot must remain on floor
    • Maximum 3 tricks/dips/flares
    • Maximum 8 bars continuous turns
    • 80% recognizable style music required
  • Team Divisions:
    • Lifts and overheads permitted
    • 50% salsa dancing required
    • Amateur teams: all amateur dancers
    • Pro Am teams: maximum 50% professionals
